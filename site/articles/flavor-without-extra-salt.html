Flavor Without Extra Salt: Herbs, Spices, and Other Tricks

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026 · 5 min read

Many of us are trying to cut back on salt, whether by choice or on a doctor’s advice. The good news is that a well-stocked spice rack and a few kitchen tricks can make food taste wonderful with less salt. Here are the techniques we rely on.

This article offers general cooking tips, not medical advice. If you follow a sodium-restricted diet, follow the guidance of your doctor or a registered dietitian.

Brighten with acid

A squeeze of lemon or lime juice, or a splash of vinegar, wakes up flavors in much the same way salt does. Try a little lemon juice in soups just before serving, or a dash of cider vinegar in a pot of beans or greens.

Build flavor with aromatics

  • Cook onions slowly until golden to bring out their natural sweetness.
  • Use plenty of fresh garlic, ginger, celery, and bell peppers.
  • Brown meats well. That golden crust adds deep, savory flavor.

Herbs and spices that pair well

FoodTry
ChickenThyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon zest, paprika
BeefBlack pepper, garlic, onion, thyme, bay leaf, mustard
FishDill, lemon, parsley, ginger, chives
PotatoesRosemary, chives, garlic, smoked paprika
Tomato dishesBasil, oregano, garlic, a pinch of red pepper
Beans and soupsCumin, smoked paprika, bay leaf, thyme
VegetablesLemon, garlic, nutmeg (for greens), dill

Shop smart

  • Choose low-sodium broth and “no salt added” canned tomatoes and beans. You can always add a pinch of salt at the end.
  • Rinse canned beans and vegetables, which removes a good portion of the sodium.
  • Read labels on seasoning blends. Many contain a lot of salt. Look for salt-free blends.
  • Use cheese, bacon, and soy sauce as accents, not main ingredients.

Season at the end

Taste your food just before serving and add salt only if it needs it. You will often find it needs much less than the recipe suggests, especially if you have used herbs, citrus, and well-browned aromatics.

Recipes that are full of flavor from herbs and citrus include our lemon herb roasted chicken, black bean and corn salad, and baked feta and tomato pasta.
