How this plan works

Sunday’s roast chicken starts the week, and leftovers make easy lunches. Monday’s lasagna soup and Friday’s sausage and kale soup share Italian sausage and chicken broth, so one shopping trip covers both. Tuesday’s salmon bowls are quick and light, Wednesday brings cozy meatballs in gravy, and Thursday’s baked feta pasta mostly cooks itself. Assemble the French toast on Friday night, and Saturday brunch just needs to be baked.

Cooking for two? Use the Half button on each recipe card and halve the shopping list amounts. For make-ahead tips, read How to Freeze and Reheat Casseroles, Soups, and Leftovers.
