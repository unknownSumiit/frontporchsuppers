Fudgy Homemade Brownies

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

Rich, fudgy, one-bowl brownies with a shiny, crackly top. Better than any boxed mix.

Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Total1 hr 40 min
Makes16 brownies
Jump to Recipe

These brownies are dense, chocolatey, and fudgy in the middle, with that irresistible shiny, crackly top. They are made in one bowl with cocoa and melted chocolate, so there is very little cleanup.

Cut them small, because they are rich. Or don’t.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line an 8-inch square pan with parchment, leaving an overhang on two sides.
  2. In a large microwave-safe bowl, melt the butter and chopped chocolate in 30-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  3. Whisk in both sugars. Add the eggs and vanilla and whisk vigorously for 1 minute, until glossy.
  4. Sift in the cocoa, then add the flour and salt and fold just until combined. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Spread in the pan and bake for 22 to 26 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with moist crumbs.
  6. Cool completely in the pan, then lift out and cut into squares.

Tips for success

  • Whisking the eggs and sugar well is what creates the shiny, crackly top.
  • For clean cuts, chill the cooled brownies for 30 minutes and wipe the knife between slices.

Storing and reheating

Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
