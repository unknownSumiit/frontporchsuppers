Classic Pumpkin Pie

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

A smooth, perfectly spiced pumpkin pie with a flaky crust. The essential Thanksgiving dessert.

Prep15 min
Cook55 min
Total3 hr 10 min
Makes8 slices
Jump to Recipe

Thanksgiving would not be the same without pumpkin pie. This classic recipe has a silky, custardy filling, warmly spiced with cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and cloves, in a flaky crust.

Make it a day ahead. It slices more neatly once chilled, and it frees up your oven on the big day.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Fit the crust into a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate and crimp the edges. Chill while you make the filling.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the pumpkin, brown sugar, eggs, and yolk until smooth.
  3. Whisk in the evaporated milk, spices, salt, and vanilla.
  4. Set the pie plate on a baking sheet and pour in the filling.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes. Reduce the heat to 350°F and bake 40 to 50 minutes more, until the edges are set and the center jiggles slightly when the pan is gently shaken.
  6. Cool on a rack for at least 2 hours, then refrigerate. Serve with whipped cream.

Tips for success

  • Do not overbake. The center firms up as it cools, and overbaking causes cracks.
  • Cover the crust edge with foil or a pie shield if it browns too fast.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate, loosely covered, for up to 4 days.
