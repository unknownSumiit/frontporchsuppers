Strawberry Rhubarb Pie

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

A sweet-tart strawberry rhubarb pie with a lattice crust. A springtime classic that tastes like Grandma’s garden.

Prep40 min
Cook1 hr
Total4 hr 40 min
Makes8 slices
Jump to Recipe

Strawberry rhubarb pie is the first pie of spring. Sweet strawberries balance the bright tartness of rhubarb, and the filling bakes into a jammy, ruby-red treat under a golden lattice.

If rhubarb is out of season, frozen rhubarb works perfectly well.

Instructions

  1. Place a rack in the lower third of the oven and heat to 400°F. Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. In a large bowl, gently toss the rhubarb, strawberries, sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt. Let stand for 10 minutes.
  3. Pour the filling into the crust and dot with the butter.
  4. Cut the second crust into 1-inch strips and weave a lattice over the filling. Trim and crimp the edges. Brush with the egg wash.
  5. Set on a foil-lined baking sheet. Bake for 20 minutes, then reduce to 375°F and bake 40 to 45 minutes more, until the filling bubbles thickly in the center.
  6. Cool completely, at least 3 hours, before slicing.

Tips for success

  • If using frozen rhubarb, do not thaw it, and add 10 minutes to the baking time.
  • A visible bubble in the center of the pie means the cornstarch has thickened properly.

Storing and reheating

Keep covered at room temperature for 1 day or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
