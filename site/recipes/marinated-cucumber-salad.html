Marinated Cucumber Onion Salad

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

A crisp, refreshing cucumber and onion salad in a sweet and tangy vinegar dressing. A summer cookout classic.

Prep15 min
Marinate1 hr
Total1 hr 15 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

This cool, crunchy cucumber salad has been on summer tables for generations. Thin slices of cucumber and onion soak in a sweet and tangy vinegar dressing until they are perfectly seasoned.

It is light, refreshing, and the ideal side for grilled chicken, burgers, or fried fish.

Instructions

  1. Place the cucumbers and onion in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk the vinegar, water, sugar, salt, and pepper until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables, add the dill, and toss well.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, stirring once or twice. Serve chilled with a slotted spoon.

Tips for success

  • English or Persian cucumbers have thin skins and small seeds, so there is no need to peel them.
  • For a creamy version, drain the cucumbers and stir in 1/2 cup sour cream before serving.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate in the dressing for up to 3 days. The cucumbers soften a little each day.
