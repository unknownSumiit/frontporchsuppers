Easy Lasagna Soup

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

All the flavors of lasagna in a cozy one-pot soup, with Italian sausage, tomatoes, broken noodles, and a dollop of cheese.

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Total50 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Lasagna soup has all the comforting flavors of a classic lasagna, the rich tomato sauce, the savory sausage, the creamy cheese, without the layering and long baking time.

It all comes together in one pot in under an hour, and a spoonful of ricotta and mozzarella on top of each bowl makes it special.

Easy Lasagna Soup

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 35 min · Total: 50 min · Serves: 6

Batch size
6 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

  • For the cheese topping

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and onion and cook, breaking up the sausage, until browned, about 8 minutes. Drain off excess fat.
  2. Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, basil, and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes and broth. Bring to a boil.
  4. Add the broken noodles and simmer, stirring occasionally, until tender, 10 to 12 minutes.
  5. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Mix the ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Ladle the soup into bowls and top each with a spoonful of the cheese mixture.

Tips for success

  • Noodles keep absorbing broth as the soup sits. If you expect leftovers, cook the noodles separately and add them to each bowl.
  • Ground beef or ground turkey can replace the sausage. Add 1/2 teaspoon fennel seed for that sausage flavor.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days, adding a splash of broth when reheating. Freeze the soup without noodles for up to 3 months.
