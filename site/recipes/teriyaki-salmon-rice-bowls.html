Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowls

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

Tender glazed salmon over rice with crisp cucumber, avocado, and edamame. A fresh, colorful dinner in 30 minutes.

Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Total30 min
Makes4 bowls
Jump to Recipe

These rice bowls are as pretty as they are easy. Salmon bakes in a homemade teriyaki glaze while the rice cooks, then everything is arranged over warm rice with fresh vegetables.

It is a light, satisfying dinner, and everyone can build their own bowl.

Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowls

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 15 min · Total: 30 min · Makes: 4 bowls

Batch size
4 bowls

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

  • For the teriyaki glaze
  • For the bowls

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine the soy sauce, honey, vinegar, ginger, and garlic. Bring to a simmer, stir in the cornstarch mixture, and cook until thickened, about 1 minute.
  3. Place the salmon on the baking sheet and brush with about half of the glaze.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and reaches 145°F.
  5. Divide the rice among four bowls. Top with the salmon, cucumber, avocado, and edamame.
  6. Drizzle with the remaining glaze and sprinkle with green onions and sesame seeds.

Tips for success

  • Store-bought teriyaki sauce works in a pinch. Use about 2/3 cup.
  • Microwavable rice pouches make this dinner even quicker.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate salmon and rice separately for up to 2 days. Slice avocado fresh when serving.
