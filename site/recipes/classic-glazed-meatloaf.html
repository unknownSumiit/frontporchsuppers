Classic Glazed Meatloaf

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

A moist, tender meatloaf with a sweet and tangy brown sugar ketchup glaze. Comfort food at its best.

Prep15 min
Cook1 hr
Total1 hr 25 min
Makes8 slices
Jump to Recipe

Meatloaf is the definition of comfort food, and this one is just how it should be: moist and tender, well seasoned, and topped with a sticky, sweet and tangy glaze.

Leftovers make the best sandwiches the next day, on soft bread with a little extra ketchup.

Classic Glazed Meatloaf

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 1 hr · Total: 1 hr 25 min · Makes: 8 slices

Batch size
8 slices

Ingredients

  • For the meatloaf
  • For the glaze

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
  2. In a large bowl, stir the breadcrumbs and milk together and let stand for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the beef, eggs, onion, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Mix gently with your hands just until combined.
  4. Shape into a 9 by 5-inch loaf on the baking sheet.
  5. Whisk the glaze ingredients together. Spread half of the glaze over the meatloaf.
  6. Bake for 45 minutes. Spread with the remaining glaze and bake 15 to 20 minutes more, until the center reaches 160°F.
  7. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

Tips for success

  • Baking the loaf on a sheet pan instead of in a loaf pan lets the fat drain away and gives more delicious glazed edges.
  • An instant-read thermometer is the surest way to know your meatloaf is done.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze slices for up to 3 months. Reheat covered at 325°F.
