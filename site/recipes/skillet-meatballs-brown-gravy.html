Skillet Meatballs in Brown Gravy

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

Tender homemade meatballs simmered in a rich brown gravy. Serve over egg noodles or mashed potatoes.

Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Total40 min
Serves4
Jump to Recipe

These tender meatballs are browned in a skillet, then simmered in a savory brown gravy until they are juicy and full of flavor. It is a cozy, old-fashioned supper that comes together in about 40 minutes.

Spoon them over buttered egg noodles or mashed potatoes so none of that gravy goes to waste.

Skillet Meatballs in Brown Gravy

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 25 min · Total: 40 min · Serves: 4

Batch size
4 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

  • For the meatballs
  • For the gravy

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix the breadcrumbs and milk and let sit for 2 minutes. Add the beef, egg, onion, garlic, salt, and pepper and mix gently with your hands. Shape into 16 meatballs.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Brown the meatballs on all sides, about 8 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Melt the butter in the same skillet. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute, until lightly browned.
  4. Slowly whisk in the broth and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer until thickened, about 3 minutes.
  5. Return the meatballs to the skillet. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes, until cooked through (160°F).
  6. Stir in the sour cream if using, warm gently, and sprinkle with parsley.

Tips for success

  • Mix the meat mixture only until combined. Overworking makes meatballs tough.
  • Wet your hands with cold water to keep the mixture from sticking as you roll.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate in the gravy for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
