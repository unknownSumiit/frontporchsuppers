Baked Feta and Tomato Pasta

By the Front Porch Suppers team · Updated November 22, 2026

Cherry tomatoes and a block of feta roast together into a creamy, tangy sauce for pasta. Simple, fresh, and ready in 40 minutes.

Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Serves4
Jump to Recipe

This easy pasta became famous for good reason. A block of feta and a pan of cherry tomatoes roast together until the tomatoes burst and the cheese turns soft and creamy. Stir it all together and you have a bright, rich sauce.

Toss in hot pasta and fresh basil, and dinner is ready with almost no effort.

Baked Feta and Tomato Pasta

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 30 min · Total: 40 min · Serves: 4

Batch size
4 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. In a 9 by 13-inch baking dish, toss the tomatoes and garlic with the olive oil, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes.
  3. Nestle the block of feta in the center and spoon some of the oil over it.
  4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the tomatoes have burst and the feta is soft and lightly golden.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted water until tender. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water, then drain.
  6. Mash the feta and tomatoes together with a fork into a creamy sauce. Add the pasta, basil, and lemon zest and toss, adding pasta water as needed to loosen.

Tips for success

  • Use a block of feta, not crumbles. It softens into a much creamier sauce.
  • Add a few handfuls of baby spinach or cooked chicken with the pasta for a heartier meal.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat with a splash of water, or enjoy cold as a pasta salad.
